MEDIUM No Account Lockout or Rate Limiting on Repeated Failed Logins ASTF-API2-2023

Endpoint: POST /api/v1/auth/logout

8 consecutive failed login attempts against the same account produced no rate-limiting or lockout response (HTTP 429/423). This allows unrestricted brute-force and credential-stuffing attacks against user accounts.

Evidence: Last of 8 attempts returned HTTP 404 with no lockout signal

Remediation: Implement account lockout or exponential rate limiting after a small number of failed attempts (e.g. 5), and consider CAPTCHA or IP-based throttling for repeated failures.
